Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 · SolenLabs LLC
The short version: Drava collects your email to give you access and send briefings. We don't sell your data, don't share it with third parties for advertising, and don't store photos you upload for analysis. The AI coaching is powered by Anthropic's Claude — your conversations are not used to train AI models.

1. Who We Are

Drava Field Guide is operated by SolenLabs LLC, a Washington State software company. When this policy says "Drava," "we," "us," or "our," it means SolenLabs LLC.

Contact: support@drava.app

2. What We Collect

Email address. When you activate your free trial, you provide your email. We use it to send your access credentials and optional weekly field briefings. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Your onboarding goal. When you select your mission objective during onboarding (e.g., "find the right woman," "level up"), we store this alongside your email to personalize communications.

Device identifier. Drava creates a random device ID (in the Android app, an app-specific ID) and stores it with your trial and purchase status. We use it to unlock what you've paid for, restore purchases, and enforce daily usage limits that prevent abuse.

Purchase history. When you subscribe or buy in the Android app, Google Play processes the payment. We receive and store the purchase token, product and expiry date so we can verify the purchase and keep your access active. On the web, Stripe processes payments.

Usage data. We may collect anonymous, aggregated data about how features are used (e.g., which tools are most popular). This data cannot be linked back to you.

What we do NOT collect: We do not collect your name, phone number, location or payment card details (payments are handled by Google Play or Stripe), and we do not store the content of your AI coaching conversations — it is processed in real time only (see section 4).

3. Photos and Images

When you use Profile Intel (photo ranking) or share a chat screenshot with the Text Coach, photos are sent directly to Anthropic's API for analysis and are not stored by Drava. Photos are processed in real time and immediately discarded after the analysis response is returned.

By using these features, you confirm that you have the right to submit the photos you upload — for example, photos that a person has shared publicly on a dating profile. You agree not to upload private photos obtained without consent.

4. AI Coaching Conversations

Your coaching conversations — including messages, threads and profile text you paste in — are processed by Anthropic's Claude AI via a secure server-side proxy. Conversation content is used only to generate the reply and is not stored in our database. Anthropic's own privacy policy governs how they handle API requests — your conversations are not used to train Anthropic's models under their standard API agreement.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain your access to Drava
  • To send you field briefings and product updates (you can opt out anytime)
  • To improve our products and features using aggregated, anonymous data
  • To respond to support requests

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with advertisers. We share data only with:

  • Supabase — our database provider (stores your email, device ID and purchase status)
  • Google Play — processes in-app purchases in the Android app
  • Anthropic — processes AI coaching requests (does not store conversations)
  • Stripe — processes subscription payments (we never see your card number)

7. Data Retention

Your email address is retained for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion at any time by visiting our Account Deletion page or emailing support@drava.app. We will delete your data within 30 days of a verified request.

8. Security

Your data is stored in Supabase's secure, encrypted database infrastructure. AI requests are routed through a server-side proxy so your API interactions are never exposed client-side. We use HTTPS for all data in transit.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under GDPR, CCPA, or other privacy laws, including the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email support@drava.app.

10. Children

Drava is intended for users 18 years of age and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted data, contact us immediately.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date at the top and notify users via email where appropriate. Continued use of Drava after changes constitutes acceptance.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@drava.app
